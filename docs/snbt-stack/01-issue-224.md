## Architecture decision
The authoritative representation of observed Minecraft storage is versioned canonical SNBT, derived through native registry-aware ItemStack serialization and validated before persistence. JSON is allowed for transport envelopes and derived projections only. Preserve typed tags and full component patches, ordered slot paths, source identity, observation freshness and authorization. Manager serves already observed storage from its own database without calling the agent. Every managed shulker receives a physically verified `WeSort-<UUID>` name automatically; internal moves keep it, verified external delivery removes it and retires the UUID. Unmarked or uncertain inventory cannot be allocated. No legacy migration. These are requirements, not claims of existing behavior.
